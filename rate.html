How to rate (read this first)

A question's number says roughly how many teams at an average online invitational would get it right more than half the time, if no other team were buzzing, with the normal clock (5 seconds after a toss-up, 20 for a bonus). Picture the whole field: a few national-level teams, a solid middle, and many newer teams.

  • Calculations: if a player knows exactly what to do with every number the moment the question is read (one formula, two quick steps), rate it like knowing that formula: low. Awkward formulas, an insight, or several steps make it climb fast.
  • Multiple choice is easier than the same thing as short answer. Length or a story setup doesn't make a question hard.
  • Answers in parts, select-all and ordering: a team must get every part.
  • Same scale for every subject, middle school and Ocean Science Bowl included.
  • Decimals are fine (2.3), and so is "Can't judge" for a subject you don't know.
