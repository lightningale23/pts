pts offline: Science Bowl buzzer practice with no internet
==========================================================

HOW TO OPEN IT
  1. Unzip this folder somewhere you'll keep it (Documents is fine). Keep every file in it together.
  2. Double-click pts.html. It opens in your web browser and works with Wi-Fi off.

  For its own window without browser tabs (needs Chrome or Edge):
    Mac:     double-click "Open pts (Mac).command"
             If the Mac says it can't verify the file, use pts.html instead,
             or allow it in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
    Windows: double-click "Open pts (Windows).bat"

  Tip: bookmark pts.html in your browser to open it in one click next time.

WHAT WORKS OFFLINE
  Practice (toss-ups, bonuses, mixed mode), every setting and filter, Science Bowl and
  Ocean Science Bowl, visual bonuses with their pictures, math, answer checking, read aloud
  (with the voices installed on your computer), the question bank browser, saved questions
  and stats.

NOT IN THIS COPY (they need the internet; use the website)
  Rooms, the Report button, Search Google and the online badge.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Stats, settings and saved questions stay in the browser on this computer. They are
    separate from the website's, and from other browsers on the same computer.
  - The questions are a snapshot from the day this copy was made (see Help > This offline
    copy). To get newer questions, download a new copy and put it where the old folder was,
    with the same name; your stats and saved questions carry over.
